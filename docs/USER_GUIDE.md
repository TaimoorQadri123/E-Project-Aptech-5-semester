# FitTrack User Guide

## Getting started

1. Open the app (usually `http://localhost:5173`).
2. Click **Sign up** — choose a **Name** (your unique username, e.g. `ali_khan`), email, password, confirm password, and a required profile photo.
3. Check the **privacy policy** box (read the policy link).
4. After signup, **Login** with your **name or email** and password.

You can set age, height, gender, and fitness goal later on the **Profile** page.

## Pages

### Dashboard
See today’s calories, workouts this week, recent workouts and meals, and a 7-day calorie chart. Use quick links to add data.

### Workouts
Add workouts with exercises (sets, reps, weight). Edit or delete entries. Use search and filters (category, tag, dates).

### Nutrition
Use **Day** to log meals for one date (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks), or **Range** to filter across dates — not both at once.

### Progress
Pick a metric (e.g. weight), add readings, view the chart and table. Use the time range filter on the chart.

### Analytics
**Workouts** tab: weekly counts, minutes, categories, and weight history per exercise. **Nutrition** tab: daily calories and macro split.

### Reports
Choose type (workouts, nutrition, progress), date range, download **CSV** or **PDF**.

### Find users
Search by **name** (username) or email prefix (min 2 characters). You only see name, goal, and age group — not email.

### Notifications
**Inbox:** workout, goal, and reminder messages. Mark read or delete.  
**Reminders:** set time and days; alerts appear while the app is open (optional browser notifications).

### Settings
Units (metric/imperial), light/dark theme, and which notification types are saved.

### Profile
**Name** and **email** are read-only. Edit age, gender, height, goal, target weight, profile photo, and password. **Download my data** (JSON) or **Delete my account** (needs password).

### Support
FAQ accordion and a form to send bug/question/feedback messages. See **My messages** for your tickets.

### Privacy policy
Public page at `/privacy` — what we store and your export/delete rights.

## FAQ

**I forgot if I used my name or email to login**  
Try either — both work with the same password.

**Why is my photo required at signup?**  
It personalizes your navbar and profile; you can change it later in Profile.

**Can I change my name?**  
No. Your name is your unique username and is fixed after signup.

**Reminders did not fire**  
Reminders run while FitTrack is open in the browser. Enable browser alerts on the Notifications page if you want OS pop-ups.

**How do I leave the app completely?**  
Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my account (enter password).
